* Cross-platform shared donor IDs mean repeated hybridisations of the same RNA, not an independent cohort.
* A zero cell with a full-rank design is a structural extrapolation problem, not classical collinearity.
* For the external candidate GSE27276 the 36 deposited titles do not encode donor identifiers; the official GEO record documents paired eyes and one technical replicate, so the audit's `n_donors = n_arrays` is a parsing limitation, NOT evidence of 36 independent donors. Donor IDs must be reconstructed manually before any model is fitted.
