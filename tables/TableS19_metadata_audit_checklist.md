# Table S19. Reusable metadata-audit checklist for public expression series (template and reporting schema)

**Purpose.** This table is the deliverable of the methodological re-framing. It specifies a fixed, read-only checklist to run on any public gene-expression series **before** the series is reused for a case-control contrast. It is a template, not a result table: it contains no disease-effect estimates and no transcript-level claims.

**Implementation.** The accompanying script `work/data/geo_metadata_audit.py` implements the five checks below. In the present study it produced 159 metrics over 7 series matrices (6 primary + 1 external candidate); the observed outcomes are in `results_audit/` and Table S16. Script outputs are `audit_report.csv`, `audit_checklist.csv`, `audit_records.json`, `audit_summary.json` and `README.md` under `results_audit/`.

**Status.** Read-only audit of deposited metadata. It does not fit a differential-expression model, does not validate or replicate any finding, does not establish equivalence or absence of effect, and does not substitute for an a-priori power calculation.

## Checklist

| ID | Check | Deposited fields required | Decision rule | Reported metrics | FAIL means | WARN means | Action before reuse | Applicability boundary |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| C1 | `free_text_covariates` | `!Sample_characteristics_ch1`, `!Sample_description`, `!Sample_title` | Age and sex must be recoverable as numeric / binary structured fields rather than inside unstructured free text; report `compound_free_text_cells` and structured-cell counts | `compound_free_text_cells`, `structured_age_cells`, `structured_sex_cells`, `check_free_text_covariates` | Covariates exist only in compound free-text strings; a pre-specified adjusted model cannot be reproduced from the deposit alone | Covariates are partly structured, or their provenance is ambiguous | Either restrict the analysis to structured fields and label the adjustment as unavailable, or document the parsing rule and publish it as code. Do not silently drop the covariate | Applies to any series with sample-level characteristics; a PASS does not guarantee the covariate is confounder-free |
| C2 | `group_x_ancestry_cells` | Group label + ancestry / race field, or an explicit statement that neither is deposited | Cross-tabulate group by ancestry; flag zero cells, compute phi, Pearson r, VIF, condition number and design rank | `group_x_ancestry_zero_cells`, `phi`, `pearson_r`, `vif`, `condition_number`, `design_rank`, `check_group_x_ancestry_cells` | Empty stratum makes the adjusted disease coefficient structurally non-identifiable even when the design matrix is full rank | Imbalance without a zero cell, or ancestry not deposited at all | Report the zero cell and the rank; either restrict the contrast to a balanced subset or state `ADJUSTMENT NOT POSSIBLE` on every affected test. Never interpret the adjusted coefficient causally | Requires a deposited ancestry field; absence of the field is recorded as WARN, not as evidence of balance |
| C3 | `replicate_donor_overlap` | `!Sample_title`, `!Sample_description`, `!Sample_geo_accession`, `!Series_overall_design`, supplementary relations | Compare declared donor count with array count; detect technical replicates (`rep1`/`rep2`, paired eyes), shared donor identifiers across platforms, and repeated hybridisations of the same RNA | `n_arrays`, `n_donors`, `shared_donor_ids`, `declared_design`, `check_replicate_donor_overlap` | Arrays are not independent units but would be modelled as independent | Duplicate or paired arrays are plausible but not fully resolvable from deposited fields | Collapse technical replicates to donors before fitting; treat same-Series platforms as repeated measurements, never as independent cohorts. Where pairing is unverifiable, report all plausible unit conventions separately | Donor identity may be unrecoverable; correlation-based pairing is an unverifiable assumption and must be labelled as such |
| C4 | `annotation_availability` | Platform ID, official `GPL*.annot.gz` availability, probe-to-gene column | Verify whether GEO provides an official annotation file; report mapping coverage for the series-matrix probe set | `annotation_status`, `annotation_coverage_pct`, `n_mapped`, `n_matrix_probes`, `check_annotation_availability` | No official annotation exists and no documented rebuild is available | Coverage is partial, or a local rebuild was used instead of an official file | Keep unmapped probes as missing annotation, never as negative results. If a local map is rebuilt from SOFT, publish the exact source, row count, extraction rule and its non-official status; keep such output in a separate boundary-check directory | Platform-specific; a locally rebuilt map is not equivalent to an official annotation and must not be promoted into the primary result |
| C5 | `scale_excluded_groups` | Expression value distribution, `!Sample_data_processing`, group labels including non-target arms (for example ocular hypertensive eyes) | Infer linear versus log2-like scale from min / median / max; enumerate groups that must be excluded from the pre-specified contrast | `scale_inference`, `excluded_arrays`, `exclusion_reason`, `check_scale_excluded_groups` | Scale is incompatible with the intended fold-change threshold, or an ineligible group would enter the contrast | Scale inference is ambiguous, or exclusions reduce residual df materially | Record the scale decision in the QC table and apply one transformation consistently; exclude ineligible arms before fitting and report the resulting residual df | Scale inference is descriptive; it is not a substitute for checking the original processing pipeline |

## Minimum reporting schema per series

For every reused series, the audit record should contain these fields so a reader can re-run the checklist without re-reading the deposit:

`dataset_id`, `role` (primary / external candidate), `platform_id`, `n_arrays`, `n_donors`, `declared_design`, `scale_inference`, `excluded_arrays`, `annotation_status`, `annotation_coverage_pct`, `check_free_text_covariates`, `check_group_x_ancestry_cells`, `check_replicate_donor_overlap`, `check_annotation_availability`, `check_scale_excluded_groups`, plus the quantitative diagnostics listed in C1–C5.

## Interpretation boundaries (mandatory when citing this checklist)

1. The checklist describes **fitness-for-purpose of deposited metadata**; it is not a statistical test, not a quality score for the underlying biology and not a validation of any transcript list.
2. A FAIL on C2 or C3 means the intended contrast is **not identifiable as a disease effect** under the deposited design. Reporting the non-significant result is still legitimate, but it must be phrased as "cannot exclude smaller effects" rather than "no effect", "equivalent" or "confirmed negative".
3. Post-hoc detectable effect sizes (Table S13) are descriptive and are not a test of equivalence; direction agreement counts (Table S17) are descriptive and carry no inferential test.
4. Applying the checklist to an external series is a **cross-tissue / cross-assay stress test** of the checklist, not an independent replication or a validation cohort.
5. The checklist does not remove the need for wet-laboratory validation, an independent human cohort, or an a-priori power calculation when the goal is a biological claim.
