## Interpretation boundaries

* `phi = 1` for disease vs collection and disease vs postmortem delay; adding these terms gives design rank 2 of 4, because both terms are exactly collinear with the disease indicator, so the confounding cannot be adjusted away.
* GPL2507 annotation covers 40.64% of matrix probes; unmapped probes are missing annotation, never negative results.
